Order No. 01 — our story

From a 45-seat room in Crestview to the whole Emerald Coast.

Bamboo was founded in October 2007 by Joe Rice and Danny Ledford after years of working in restaurants in Destin. They wanted to bring real sushi and real hibachi to the area at a price people could actually pay on a Tuesday — starting small, with a 45-seat room in Crestview.

Word got around fast. Crestview expanded, then Fort Walton Beach opened, then Niceville in 2015. Three locations later, it’s still the same family behind the bar and the line — fast-paced, a little loud, and genuinely fun.

“Come in as a customer and leave as a friend.”

Order No. 03 — how it works

Full-service care, counter-service speed.

Order at the register, grab a table tent, and sit wherever you like. Our runners and bar staff take it from there — drinks, refills, anything you need. No waiting to be seated, no waiting on a check at the end.
