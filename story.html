Our Story

Bamboo started in Crestview with a simple idea: make sushi and hibachi approachable, fresh and part of the local community.

Where it started

Local from day one.

Bamboo was founded in October 2007 by Joe Rice and Danny Ledford after years of restaurant work in Destin. The first restaurant opened as a 45-seat room in Crestview.

As the restaurant grew, Bamboo expanded to Fort Walton Beach and later Niceville while remaining family-owned and rooted on Florida's Emerald Coast.
