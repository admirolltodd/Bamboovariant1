Our Story

Bamboo began in Crestview and grew across the Emerald Coast without losing the local, family-owned identity that started it.

Where it started

Local from day one.

Bamboo was founded in October 2007 by Joe Rice and Danny Ledford after years of restaurant work in Destin. The first restaurant opened as a 45-seat room in Crestview.

As Bamboo grew, it expanded to Fort Walton Beach and later Niceville while remaining family-owned and rooted on Florida's Emerald Coast.
